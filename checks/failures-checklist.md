# Failures checklist

Built from the mistakes logged in the game's first playthroughs. The continuity checker runs it every sleep; the narrator runs it before every morning. Each check is a yes/no question about the day just played. A yes is a failure.

## Address and names
1. Was the character addressed by her husband's surname, or as "Mrs." plus anything? (She keeps her birth surname. Officials use her own surname or a role.)
2. Did Sujin call her sister by her given name outside an emphatic moment?
3. Did two characters in one scene address her two different ways?
4. Were names used where Korean would use role, relation, or nothing?

## Authorship
5. Did the narrator write words, thoughts, feelings or decisions for her that the player did not give?
6. Did the narrator invent a tactically weighted detail (contents of a note, handling of a weapon, gloves, what she told a witness) instead of asking one light question?
7. Did a player-authored detail that should have mattered get forgotten when its consequence came due?
8. Were musings treated as orders, or an action resolved before the player committed?

## Pace and shape
9. Did a turn end on a stacked menu of options instead of on the scene's pressure?
10. Did in-game hours pass as a flat toll for a question, a recollection or a conversation with the narrator?
11. Did the status line carry editorial phrases instead of plain state?
12. Was a response noticeably longer than the scene earned?
13. Did a day pass with nothing moving against her (no hunter moving, no word travelling, the clock not felt)?

## Sujin
14. Did Sujin state something the player had plainly already clocked?
15. Did Sujin mother reflexively, or caution without WEIGHT having earned it?

## The fire and the list
16. Did any discovered material lobby for restraint or make the husband read as a knowing profiteer?
17. Was proof of someone's hand in the killing reached without the sentencing beat being offered?
18. Was a name written onto the list by the narrator rather than the player?
19. Is the campaign past day 16 with violence never yet genuinely on the table?

## The record
20. Did the narrator contradict a fact in the save, the cards, the rulings or the case file?
21. Was a ruling made on a matter already ruled on, without checking the ledger?
22. Did the narrator leak a meter, a target number, a roll, a hunter's sheet, or anything from the sealed case?
23. Did a hunter act on something its inbox never gave it?
24. Was a missed consequential clue allowed to die without resurfacing through another channel?
25. Is there no lit next rung, or more than one?

## Growth
When a kind of mistake recurs that no check covers, the narrator proposes a new check in (oog); the player approves; it is added here and logged in `state/changelist.md`.
