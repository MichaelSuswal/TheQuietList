# Case brief (constraints for the case-writer)

The sealed case is written fresh for every campaign, by the case-writer agent, so that no one, the creator included, knows the specifics before play. These constraints bind every case.

## Fixed
- Seoul, late 1997, under the IMF crisis. Grounded realism.
- The husband is murdered about three days before the funeral. Police rule it a mugging on the day of the funeral. Detective Park delivers the ruling in person.
- The industry: one of redevelopment/construction, early internet/telecom, or merchant-bank lending. Money-flush, cutthroat, entangled with organized crime (tenant clearing, loan-sharking, extortion).
- The partner at the top was **coerced**, by debt or a threat to his own family, into allowing or arranging the death. He did not swing the weapon. Not innocent, not completely guilty.
- The chain of hands between the partner and the body: three or four people, each more formidable and more forewarned than the one below.
- One paid officer at the bottom of the police side; someone above him who signed off.
- The 49th day is the wall: by then the partner must be beyond reach if she has not arrived.

## The husband (absolute)
- He **loved her, completely, never in doubt**.
- He was **drawn in, never a knowing profiteer**: deceived about what he was handling, leveraged once he saw it, or entangled while protecting someone (his family, her).
- His failings are of judgment and of love: too slow to see, too slow to get out, hid the danger to spare her.
- Everything he left behind makes her love him more. No confession of greed, no document that reads as "he had it coming".
- The twist complicates the facts of his death and the business, never his love and never his decency.

## The mystery standard
- Every truth has a body: a place, a person, a paper.
- Clues are never held back; a missed clue resurfaces through a different channel.
- Every open thread has a cost tied to the clock.
- Reasons never change mid-campaign. Once written, the case is the case.
- No one in the world is fully right about the cause until she proves it.
